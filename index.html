About Me

The Artist Behind
the Needle

With over a decade of experience in the art of tattooing, I've dedicated my life to transforming ideas into permanent works of art. Every piece I create is a collaboration between artist and canvas — your story, my craft.

My journey began in the traditional tattoo world, but I've since expanded my repertoire to include realism, blackwork, Japanese, and geometric styles. I believe that a great tattoo isn't just about technical skill — it's about understanding the person wearing it.
